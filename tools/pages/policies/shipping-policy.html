Shipping Policy

Delivery Times

Orders are typically processed within 1–2 business days and delivered within 4–7 business days across India, depending on your location.

Shipping Charges

We offer free shipping on all prepaid orders above ₹1,999. Orders below this amount incur a flat shipping fee of ₹99.

Order Tracking

Once your order ships, you'll receive a tracking link by email and SMS. You can also check your order status on our Track Order page.

Delays

While we aim to meet the timelines above, deliveries may occasionally be delayed due to courier or regional factors beyond our control. We'll keep you informed if this happens.

Questions

For anything shipping-related, reach out any time via our Contact page.
