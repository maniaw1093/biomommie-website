Frequently Asked Questions

How do I know what size to order?+

Use our Size Guide, based on your baby's height and age. If you're between sizes, we generally recommend sizing up.

What payment methods do you accept?+

We accept all major cards, UPI, net banking and popular wallets through our secure Razorpay checkout.

How long does delivery take?+

Most orders are delivered within 4–7 business days. See our Shipping Policy for full details.

Can I return or exchange an item?+

Yes — unused items with tags attached can be returned within 7 days of delivery. See our Return Policy.

Do you offer gift wrapping?+

Yes — every order can be gift-wrapped in our signature ivory presentation, with the option to add a handwritten message. See Gifting.

How should I care for my BioMommie pieces?+

Always follow the care label on the garment. As a general guide, we recommend a gentle cold machine wash and line drying in the shade — read more in our care guide.
